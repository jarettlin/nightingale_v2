## What a fertility assessment includes
A fertility assessment usually includes a consultation about your health history, an AMH blood test, other hormone blood tests, and a pelvic ultrasound to look at the uterus and ovaries. A semen analysis may be offered for male partners.

## The AMH test
AMH (anti-Müllerian hormone) is a blood test that gives an estimate of ovarian reserve, meaning roughly how many eggs remain. It does not on its own tell you whether you can get pregnant naturally. AMH can be tested on any day of the cycle.

## When people usually seek testing
Most couples who are trying to conceive do so within a year. People often seek a fertility assessment after 12 months of trying to conceive, or after 6 months if they are 35 or older, or sooner if they have irregular periods or a known condition.
