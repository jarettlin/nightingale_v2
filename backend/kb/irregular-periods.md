# Irregular periods: general information

> Synthetic demo content for the Nightingale build. General education only, not a diagnosis.

## What counts as irregular
Cycles are often described as irregular when their length varies a lot from month to month, when they are shorter than about 21 days or longer than about 35 days, or when periods stop for several months.

## Common reasons
Irregular cycles can be linked to many things, including stress, changes in weight, thyroid conditions, polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS), and the approach of menopause. Only a doctor can work out the reason for a particular person.

## When to talk to a doctor
It is worth seeing a doctor if your periods have become irregular, if they stop for three months or more, or if you are trying to conceive and your cycles are irregular. Very heavy bleeding, bleeding with dizziness, or severe pain needs urgent care.
