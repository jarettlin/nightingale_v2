# Opening hours, location and booking

> Synthetic demo content for the Nightingale build. Not a real clinic.

## Opening hours
The clinic is open Monday to Friday from 8:30am to 5:30pm, and Saturday from 9am to 1pm. We are closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## Location
We are on Level 3, Sunrise Medical Centre, Jalan Ampang, Kuala Lumpur. Parking is available in the building basement.

## How to book
You can book a first consultation through this chat by continuing securely, or by calling the front desk during opening hours. First consultations usually take about 45 minutes.

## Replies from the nurse team
Messages sent to the clinic through Nightingale are read by our nurse team, who usually reply within 12 to 18 hours. Messages are not monitored overnight, so in an emergency call 999.
