# Your first visit

> Synthetic demo content for the Nightingale build. Not a real clinic.

## What to expect
At your first consultation, a fertility doctor talks with you about your health, your cycles and your goals, and explains which tests may help. Many people have an ultrasound scan on the same day.

## What to bring
Please bring a list of any medicines or supplements you take, any previous test results, and your identity card. If you have a partner involved in treatment, they are welcome to attend.

## Cost of a first consultation
A first consultation costs RM 250 to RM 350, not including tests. Prices are a guide and are confirmed when you book.
