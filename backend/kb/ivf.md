## What IVF is
In IVF, eggs are collected from the ovaries and fertilised with sperm in a laboratory. A resulting embryo is then placed in the uterus.

## How the process works
IVF begins with hormone injections to stimulate the ovaries, usually for 10 to 14 days, with ultrasound monitoring. Eggs are collected under sedation and fertilised in the lab. An embryo transfer usually happens 3 to 5 days later, and a pregnancy test is done about two weeks after transfer.
