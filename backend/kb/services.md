# Services at Sunrise Fertility Clinic

> Synthetic demo content for the Nightingale build. Not a real clinic.

## Services we offer
Sunrise Fertility Clinic offers fertility assessments for individuals and couples, AMH and hormone blood tests, pelvic ultrasound scans, egg freezing, IVF (in vitro fertilisation), IUI (intrauterine insemination), and follow-up consultations with a fertility doctor.

## Services we do not offer
We do not offer sperm freezing, donor eggs, donor sperm or surrogacy. We can refer you to partner centres for these.

## Who we see
We see adults of any relationship status. A GP referral is welcome but not required to book a first consultation.
