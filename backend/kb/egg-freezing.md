## What egg freezing is
Egg freezing (oocyte cryopreservation) collects eggs from the ovaries and freezes them so they can be thawed and used for IVF in the future.

## How the process works
Treatment usually starts with about 10 to 14 days of daily hormone injections to help several eggs mature. During this time you have regular ultrasound scans and blood tests. The eggs are then collected in a short procedure under sedation, and frozen the same day.

## How long it takes
From the first injection to egg collection usually takes around two weeks. Most people return to normal activities within a day or two of collection.

## Age and success
Egg quality and number decline with age, so eggs frozen at a younger age generally have a better chance of leading to a pregnancy later. Freezing eggs does not guarantee a future pregnancy. A fertility doctor can discuss what to expect for your situation.
